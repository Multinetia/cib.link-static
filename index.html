How itworks

1
Tell us about your projects, we will select the one with the biggest disruption potential.

2
We organize a commando mission with you and our Task Force that will bring your project to life in few days.

Definitions

Run

A 3 to 4 days commando mission (5 ot 6 days from arrival to departure) development iteration of your product with our expert team, who will live and work all day long with your own team, in an amazing, unique place.

Each run is a delivery iteration of functionnal product, to get feedback ASAP.

Minimum Viable Product
